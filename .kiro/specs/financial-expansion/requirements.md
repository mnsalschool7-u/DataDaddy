# Financial and geospatial expansion

These are implementation records added by Codex; existing Kiro artifacts are preserved. They do not imply Kiro executed these changes.

- WHEN a user drags a facility token onto the map, THE SYSTEM SHALL unproject the drop position into coordinates, display an acreage-based conceptual footprint, analysis radius and relocation marker, and open financial analysis.
- WHEN project demand, financing, developer funding, rent sensitivity, household income or an institutional assumption changes, THE SYSTEM SHALL recompute deterministic outputs immediately.
- WHEN capacity is missing, THE SYSTEM SHALL show unknown; WHEN a project exceeds explicitly entered capacity, THE SYSTEM SHALL show a red scenario constraint without claiming verified utility saturation.
- THE SYSTEM SHALL support seven map analysis modes, with financial overlays limited to the selected municipality and insufficient evidence represented by hatching.
- THE SYSTEM SHALL distinguish actual 2020 Census housing counts/density and historical 2016 land-use intersections from household assumptions and hypothetical housing-market sensitivity.
- THE SYSTEM SHALL calculate household, institutional and municipal sensitivities with editable inputs, bounded allocations and visible formulas; positive, zero and negative rent sensitivities are supported.
- THE SYSTEM SHALL compare conventional, green and community-optimized variants and low/medium/high infrastructure costs.
- THE SYSTEM SHALL export financial assumptions, calculations, variants and sources into the existing printable report and ground analyst explanations in calculated outputs.
- Unavailable ACS rent/income, present-day tariffs, current parcel availability, measured displacement, generator emissions and documented legal exclusions SHALL NOT be fabricated.
