# DataDaddy — Massachusetts Policy Steering

Official references only. Do not invent laws, thresholds, or applicability.

## Primary source — Executive Order No. 658 (Sept 8, 2026)
"Establishing Requirements for Responsible Data Center Development and Operations in
Massachusetts to Protect and Support Ratepayers, Communities, and the Environment."
https://www.mass.gov/executive-orders/no-658-establishing-requirements-for-responsible-data-center-development-and-operations-in-massachusetts-to-protect-and-support-ratepayers-communities-and-the-environment

Scope touches: large projects exceeding 25 MW peak demand; community benefits agreements;
environmental & public-health requirements; clean-electricity procurement; water quantity
& quality; ratepayer protection; infrastructure cost allocation; reporting/disclosure.
Also addresses expansions and allows specific exemptions (e.g., certain university-related
facilities approved under the stated process).

## Threshold logic (exact)
- 20 MW: below the >25 MW threshold.
- 25 MW: NOT automatically above (threshold is *greater than* 25 MW).
- 30 MW: above the threshold, subject to scope and other facts.
- Expansions: use a separate applicability check reflecting the order's language.
